LUVYO Privacy Policy

LUVYO – Philippine Mobility App · Effective date: [set before publishing] · Contact: [your support email]

LUVYO is designed to comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and National Privacy Commission issuances.

What we collect

  • Without an account: saved places, routes, history, expenses and your contributions are stored only on your device.
  • Location: used only when you tap a location action (current location, nearby stops, step-by-step progress, ride recording). We do not collect location in the background.
  • Ride recordings: recorded only while the recording screen is open. The first and last 300 m are removed on your device before anything is shared, so your home or workplace is not revealed.
  • Community reports: the fare, waiting time, route or photo you submit, an approximate location (about 11 m precision) and a pseudonymous contributor ID.
  • Accounts (when offered): email, display name and preferences, protected so only you can read them.

How we use it

To plan routes, estimate fares and travel times, and let riders verify each other's reports. Aggregated, anonymous counts (such as popular destinations) help improve coverage. We do not sell personal data and we do not show personalised ads.

Sharing

Opening a ride-hailing or motorcycle-taxi provider takes you to that provider's own app or website, where their privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can view, correct, export (Profile → Export my data) and delete (Profile → Delete my account & data) your information at any time. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device. For other requests, contact us at the address above.

Children

LUVYO is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
